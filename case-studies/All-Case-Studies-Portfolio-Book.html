1. Subscription Model Redesign

Transforming a declining business model through strategic subscription architecture.

Top Synergy Management Phils. Inc. (Green Crew House Cleaning Services)
Sales & Marketing Officer
May 2023 – Feb 2024

Executive Summary

This case study demonstrates how systematic problem analysis and solution design can transform a struggling B2B service business. By identifying the root cause of 70% client churn and designing a tiered subscription model, I increased client retention by 90%+, established predictable revenue streams, and created the foundation for scalable growth.

Key Results

  • 70% churn → near 0% during contract periods
  • 100% pilot adoption (5/5 early clients)
  • 72% full rollout success (18/25 target accounts)
  • 50% revenue increase from subscription model
  • 90%+ renewal rates on recurring contracts

The Problem

70% Annual Client Churn

Green Crew's client base consisted of one-time buyers. After the initial cleaning, clients either didn't think they needed another deep clean, forgot about the service, shopped around for competitors, or managed cleaning in-house.

Revenue Unpredictability

With 70% churn, the company couldn't forecast revenue. Management couldn't confidently plan inventory, staffing, or growth investments.

Unsustainable Growth Model

The company relied entirely on customer acquisition. High-touch sales efforts were required for every booking. Scaling required proportionally more sales resources.

Solution Design

Tiered Subscription Model

  • Starter: Deep clean every 2 months (6 cleans/year)
  • Growth: Deep clean monthly (12 cleans/year)
  • Scale: Deep clean every 2 weeks (26 cleans/year)

Contract terms of 3, 6, or 12 months with discounted per-clean pricing for longer commitments (10-15% discount on 12-month). Automatic renewal unless client opts out 14 days before expiration.

Implementation & Results

Phase 1 (Pilot): Approached 5 existing high-value clients with the subscription offer. Results: 100% pilot adoption.

Phase 2 (Rollout): Expanded to 25 target accounts. Results: 72% conversion rate (18/25).

Quantified Outcomes

  • Churn Rate: 70% → ~5%
  • Revenue Stability: 50% increase in recurring revenue
  • Client Lifetime Value: Increased 3x
  • Client Renewal Rates: 90%+

2. Dashboard Design & Optimization

Transforming a 45-second dashboard load time into a 5-second powerhouse through system redesign.

Full Feeling Inc. (Dohtonbori Philippines)
Digital Marketing & PR Officer
Mar 2024 – Jun 2024

Executive Summary

This case study demonstrates how business analysis principles—requirements gathering, system optimization, and iterative refinement—can dramatically improve reporting infrastructure and stakeholder adoption. By conducting systematic stakeholder interviews, redesigning Excel architecture, and optimizing data workflows, I reduced dashboard load time by 90% while increasing user adoption from 42% to 87%.

Key Results

  • 90% performance improvement: 45 seconds → 5 seconds load time
  • 86% file size reduction: 85MB → 12MB
  • 105% adoption increase: 42% → 87% monthly active users
  • Data quality standards established across branches

The Problem

  • 45-second load time: Unacceptable user experience
  • 85MB file size: Too large; slow transmission; prone to corruption
  • 50+ interconnected sheets: Complex dependencies; difficult to troubleshoot
  • Manual data entry points: High error rates

Solution & Implementation

Architecture Redesign

  • Data Layer: 3 source files with automated overnight refresh
  • Processing Layer: Consolidated data sheet with validation checks
  • Presentation Layer: 3 separate role-based dashboards

Performance Optimization

  • Removed historical data older than 2 years
  • Optimized formulas (INDEX/MATCH vs VLOOKUP)
  • Automated overnight refresh
  • Pre-calculated summaries instead of live pivot tables

Results & Business Impact

Quantified Outcomes

  • Performance: 45 seconds → 5 seconds (90% improvement)
  • File Size: 85MB → 12MB (86% reduction)
  • User Adoption: 42% → 87% (2x increase)
  • Data Quality: Validation rules caught 15-20 errors per month

Requirements Gathering Stakeholder Management Dashboard Design System Optimization Data Quality Management

4. Customer Research & Insight Consultant

Crescent Shopping Centre, Limerick
TUS Master's Live Project
Jan 2025 – Apr 2025
A (93/100)

Executive Summary

This Master's live project demonstrates advanced customer research and insight analysis. Working independently with a real-world client, I designed and conducted a 305-respondent customer survey, analyzed data using statistical methods (IBM SPSS), and delivered actionable insights that directly informed a €2.3 million sustainability investment strategy.

Key Results

  • 305 survey respondents from diverse customer base
  • 3 customer segments identified via cluster analysis
  • 72% of customers demand EV charging infrastructure
  • 68% seek improved recycling access
  • €2.3M sustainability investment informed by research
  • Grade: A (93/100)

Research Methodology

Survey Design

Designed 25-question online survey via Microsoft Forms covering shopping patterns, sustainability priorities, willingness to pay, and demographics.

Sampling Strategy

  • Distributed QR codes at shopping center entrances
  • Promoted via center's social media and email
  • Achieved 305 responses over 6-week period

Data Analysis (IBM SPSS)

  • Descriptive statistics and satisfaction ratings
  • Cross-tabulation analysis by demographics
  • K-means cluster analysis to identify segments
  • Chi-square tests for statistical significance

Customer Segments Identified

Segment 1: Eco-Conscious Premium (25%)

  • High sustainability importance (9.2/10)
  • Support ALL investments
  • Willing to pay premium
  • Brand ambassadors; high lifetime value

Segment 2: Practical Convenience (45%)

  • Moderate sustainability interest (6.5/10)
  • High interest in EV charging
  • Largest segment; EV charging appeals

Segment 3: Price-Focused (30%)

  • Low sustainability importance (3.8/10)
  • Motivated by deals and convenience
  • Focus marketing on deal-based value

Strategic Recommendations & Outcomes

Investment Priority

  • Phase 1: EV Charging infrastructure (highest demand: 72%)
  • Phase 2: Enhanced recycling (68% demand, high ROI)
  • Phase 3: Sustainable food court
  • Phase 4: Renewable energy

Client Outcomes

  • €300K investment approved for EV charging — 8 stations installed Q2 2025

5. B2B Commercial Intelligence Consultant

Lights & Luminations Inc. (US-based lighting wholesaler)
TUS Master's Live Project
Oct 2024 – Dec 2024
A (94/100)

Executive Summary

This Master's live project demonstrates advanced business intelligence and strategic market analysis. Working independently with a real US-based B2B wholesaler, I analyzed £6.2M in live sales data across UK markets using Phocas BI, identified six distinct market segments with varying growth potential, and delivered a strategic roadmap to achieve the client's ambitious 100% revenue growth target.

Key Results

  • £6.2M revenue analyzed across UK market
  • 6 market segments developed with distinct profiles
  • 100% growth strategy recommended with 48% achievable path identified
  • Q1 2025 validation: 22% actual growth achieved
  • Smart Connected products: +35% growth (predicted segment delivery)
  • Grade: A (94/100)

The Business Challenge

Lights & Luminations Inc. is a US-based lighting wholesaler seeking to double UK "Outside & Garden" lighting sales within one year (100% growth target for 2025).

The challenge: With 3 years of historical sales data, the company lacked strategic insight into which customer segments were most valuable, which regions had highest potential, which product categories were winning/losing, and which segments could realistically double purchasing.

Analytical Approach Using Phocas BI

Data Foundation

  • 3 years of UK sales transactions (2021-2024)
  • Customer types, size, location dimensions
  • Product categories and price tiers
  • Geographic regions (8 UK areas)
  • Metrics: revenue, units, margin, repeat purchase rate

Segmentation Dimensions

  • Dimension 1: Customer Type Analysis (revenue, margin, loyalty)
  • Dimension 2: Geographic Analysis (penetration, growth)
  • Dimension 3: Product Category Analysis (performance, pairing)

Six Market Segments Identified

Segment A: Premium Property Professionals

  • Current Revenue: £2.1M (highest value)
  • Growth Potential: 15% (mature segment)
  • Strategy: Consultative sales, long-term contracts

Segment B: Growth Contractors

  • Current Revenue: £2.1M (volume leader)
  • Growth Potential: 50% (high opportunity)
  • Strategy: Volume incentives, contractor programs

Segment C: Retail & Garden Centers

  • Current Revenue: £1.4M
  • Growth Potential: 25% (retention focus)
  • Strategy: Merchandising support, exclusive lines

Segment D: Smart Tech Early Adopters

  • Current Revenue: £980K
  • Growth Potential: 60% (emerging segment)
  • Strategy: Innovation focus, integration partnerships

Segment E: Emerging Regions

  • Current Revenue: £800K
  • Growth Potential: 40% (geographic expansion)
  • Strategy: Regional hiring, local partnerships

Segment F: Declining Market

  • Current Revenue: £620K
  • Growth Potential: 0% (maintenance mode)
  • Strategy: Redirect resources to growth segments

Strategic Roadmap & Validation

Path to Target Growth

  • Current revenue: £6.2M
  • Foundation growth (45%): +£1.7M
  • Growth segment growth (50%): +£0.9M
  • Subtotal: £9.2M (48% growth)
  • Gap to 100% target: £3.2M (requires concurrent initiatives)

Q1 2025 Real-World Validation

  • Q1 2025 Revenue: +22% (ahead of projections)
  • Smart Connected products: +35% (matched Segment D prediction exactly)
  • New Wales region: 8 new landscape contractor accounts acquired (validated Segment E expansion)
  • Commission restructuring: Client realigned incentives based on segment strategy
